Making Parking Easier in Panabo City

Finding available parking can take time and cause unnecessary stress, especially when drivers arrive without knowing which parking spaces are occupied.

The Problem

Drivers often have difficulty finding available parking spaces because they cannot easily check parking availability before arriving.

Our Solution

FindMySpot provides a platform where drivers can view parking areas and check availability reported by authorized parking staff.

Our Purpose

We aim to make parking information more accessible while helping small parking establishments participate without requiring expensive sensors.

Our Prototype

Explore the FindMySpot platform and discover how drivers, parking staff, and administrators work together to make parking information more accessible.

Driver Dashboard

Drivers can search for parking areas, view reported availability, explore the map, and get directions.

Staff Dashboard

Parking staff can manage parking slots and update their status as Available or Occupied.

Admin Dashboard

Administrators can manage establishment applications, parking areas, users, and staff accounts.

Parking Availability

Drivers can view parking slots reported as Available or Occupied before heading to an establishment.

About FindMySpot
